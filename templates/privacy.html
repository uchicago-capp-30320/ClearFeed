Our Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Here's how we protect your data.

Our Commitment

ClearFeed is built on the belief that social media should be transparent — and that starts with how we handle your data. Your privacy and security are our top priority.

Important: Data sent in ClearFeed is not made public nor is it shared with a third-party service. We do not buy, sell, share, or trade user data. We do not use user data for external advertisement.

ClearFeed uses a browser extension to capture publicly visible information from your Twitter/X feed as you scroll. The captured data includes post content, author metadata (such as follower count), engagement metrics (favorites, comments, quote tweets, replies, retweets), hashtags, mentions, and whether a post is promoted. This data is sent directly from your browser to ClearFeed's servers — no intermediary services involved.

How Do We Process Your Data?

Data submitted to ClearFeed is analyzed using the following models hosted on our servers:

  • A sentiment classifier — cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest
  • A topic classifier — cardiffnlp/tweet-topic-21-multi
  • A personalized feed blurb generated by meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct

Promoted content detection and word frequency analysis are computed directly from your feed data. All models run on ClearFeed's own servers — your data is not sent to any external AI provider or third party service.

Authentication & Security

ClearFeed uses Django's built-in authentication system to manage user accounts and sessions. Passwords are hashed using Django's default PBKDF2 algorithm and never stored in plain text. Session tokens are stored as secure cookies and are used to authenticate requests from the browser extension to the server. Users can log out at any time, which invalidates their session immediately.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data. At any time, you can request a complete wipe of your user data from our database, including all analysis and associated information.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about our privacy practices or wish to request deletion of your data, please contact us.
