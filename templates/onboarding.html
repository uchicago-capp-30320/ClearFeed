Install ClearFeed

The extension runs silently in the background while you browse Twitter, capturing what appears in your feed. It never accesses your Twitter password or personal information.

Install ClearFeed Extension
Firefox will ask you to confirm the installation. Click Add. Once installed, the ClearFeed icon should appear in your Firefox toolbar.

Browse Twitter

Open Twitter/X in this same Firefox window and scroll through your home feed. The extension captures tweets automatically.

  • Scroll at your normal pace, do not rush too quickly.
  • Browse for at least 2–3 minutes for meaningful analysis.
  • The longer you scroll, the longer analysis may take.
4

Upload your data

When you are done browsing, go back to the ClearFeed Capture tab.

  1. Click To ClearFeed.
  2. Wait for Upload complete!.
Do not close the browser while uploading. If you do not want to upload your results, click Delete instead.
5

View your results

FAQ

Why does ClearFeed require Firefox?

The extension uses Firefox's WebExtension API. Chrome and Safari are not currently supported.

Does ClearFeed Capture store my Twitter password?

No. ClearFeed Capture only captures tweets that appear on your screen as you scroll. It never accesses your login credentials.

Why isn't my analysis showing yet?

Analysis runs in the background after upload. Wait about a minute and refresh the dashboard.

Can I upload multiple times?

Yes. Each upload creates a new browsing session. For best results, refresh your feed between browsing sessions.
