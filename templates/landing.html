The Problem

Social media platforms send users an endless stream of content determined by algorithms with unknown weights and functions. People scroll through hundreds of posts daily without understanding the big picture of what is being recommended to them and whether this actually aligns with their interests.

Our Solution

ClearFeed is a tool designed to give users a simple summary of who and what is showing up in their scrolling. Using a browser extension, ClearFeed collects metadata from posts and uses a local LLM to summarize content genres, common users, and flag uncomfortable topics.

Your Privacy Matters

All analysis happens locally on your device. We never store your personal data.
